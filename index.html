Features

Built for a steady posting rhythm

Drop finished videos in a folder. The uploader handles the rest, every day, without anyone opening YouTube Studio.

Drive folder as the queue

Videos are picked up in order from a Google Drive folder — no manual file handling.

Scheduled publishing

Publishes one or two videos a day at set times to keep the channel consistent.

Metadata included

Each upload gets its title, description, tags and privacy setting applied automatically.

Made for Shorts

Optimized for vertical 9:16 videos under a minute — the format the channel publishes.

Official Google sign-in

Uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API. No passwords are ever stored.

Upload log

Every upload is logged with its video ID and time, so nothing is posted twice.

How it works

Three steps, then hands-off

A one-time setup, after which the uploader runs on its own.

Connect

The channel owner signs in once with Google and grants permission to upload to their channel.

Fill the folder

Finished Shorts and their details are added to the Drive folder as they're produced.

It publishes

On schedule, the next video is uploaded to YouTube with its metadata and goes live.

Data & privacy

Your channel, your data — nothing more.

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses only what it needs to publish videos and never shares data with anyone.

  • Only uploads to the signed-in user's own channel
  • No data sold, shared or sent to third parties
  • Access can be revoked anytime from your Google Account
  • Follows the Google API Services User Data Policy
