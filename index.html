Shorts Uploader

A private automation tool that uploads videos from the owner's Google Drive to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Reads finished video files from a folder in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Uploads them to the owner's YouTube channel with a title, description and tags.
  • Runs on a schedule, publishing one or two videos per day.

Who it is for

This app is used only by its owner to manage their own channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Google API usage

The app requests access to the YouTube Data API only to upload and manage videos on the authorizing user's channel. See the Privacy Policy for details.
